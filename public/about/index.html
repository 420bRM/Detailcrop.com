About DetailCrop

Several crops per image, a whole batch in one sitting. What DetailCrop does, and why it exists.

What is Multi Crop?

A normal image crop takes one area out of one picture. A batch cropper goes a step further and cuts the same area out of many pictures at once. What we kept needing sat somewhere in between.

Say you have twenty product photos and each needs four close-ups — the fabric, a button, the label, the hem. The details sit in a different spot in every photo, so a batch cropper can't do it, and doing it one file at a time in an editor means eighty rounds of crop-and-save. Somewhere along the way a ratio or a file name slips.

DetailCrop calls its way of handling this Multi Crop:

  • Put up to six crop boxes on a single image.
  • Place them per photo, wherever the detail is.
  • Ratio, output size and naming rule apply to the whole batch.
  • When you are done, every crop comes down as one zip.

You choose where. The tool handles the repetition.

What it can do

  • Load up to 200 JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF or GIF images at once
  • Lock the ratio to 1:1, 4:5, 2:3, 3:4, 5:7, 16:9, 9:16, a custom ratio, or leave it free
  • Give one crop its own ratio, so a single image can carry a 4:5 and a 16:9
  • Straighten a tilted photo before cropping
  • Export at original pixels or at a fixed size
  • Choose WebP, JPEG or PNG and set the quality
  • Name files automatically with a pattern like {name}-d{n}
  • Split a picture into a 3, 6 or 9-post Instagram grid

Your images are never uploaded

Everything happens inside your browser. Photos are not sent to a server — there is no code that could send them. Unreleased product shots or client originals are fine to use. The only thing recorded is anonymous usage stats (image sizes, batch size, the settings you pick), and you can switch that off at the bottom of the tool.

Who uses it

  • Online shops making detail shots for every product listing
  • Artists and print sellers showing brushwork, texture or a signature up close
  • Social media teams turning one photo into a feed post, a story and a thumbnail
  • Real estate and interior studios pulling finishes and views out of a room shot

There are worked examples on what people use it for, and a step-by-step walkthrough in how to crop many images at once.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account and nothing to install.

Will it lower the quality?

By default it cuts the original pixels as they are, so nothing is lost beyond the compression of the format and quality you choose. If you set a fixed output size and a box is smaller than that, the crop gets scaled up and softens — the tool warns you when that happens.

Does it work on a phone?

It opens, but juggling several boxes is much easier on a desktop browser.

Can it crop video?

There is a video version (beta) that works the same way: several boxes on one clip, each saved as its own mp4.
