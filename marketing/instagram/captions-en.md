## 04 · Many images, one set of rules

```
Detail shots aren't about cropping one image well.
They're about cropping every image the same way.

✔️ Locked ratio: 1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 9:16 or your own
✔️ Fixed output size: every crop at the same pixels
✔️ File-name pattern: {name}-{nn} = original name + number
✔️ Format: JPEG · PNG · WebP

Set it once, and the whole lineup comes out to spec,
in one zip, straight to your downloads. 📦

## 07 · Instagram feed grid

```
Split one image into 3, 6 or 9 tiles
that line up like a puzzle on your profile grid. 🧩

The part everyone gets wrong:
your profile puts the newest post top-left,
so the bottom-right tile has to go up first.

DetailCrop numbers the tiles in posting order, not reading order,
and names the files -post01, -post02… to match.
Just upload them in order.

👉 The first posts on this account went up the same way, 09 → 01.

🔗 detailcrop.com

#DetailCrop #instagramgrid #gridpost #puzzlefeed #instagramfeed #feedplanning #instagramtips #contentplanning #socialmediatips #gridlayout
