New features and fixes, newest first.

22 October 2026

  • Fixed some crops being saved as black images when a phone ran short of memory. If a crop still cannot be made, it is left out rather than saved empty, and the export message says how many.
  • Fixed crops coming out stretched when the ratio was changed while exporting at a fixed size. Instagram feed splits now save at an exact 4:5.
  • When a phone opens a very large photo smaller than its original size, the page now tells you. For full-resolution crops, use a computer.

13 October 2026

  • Signed-in members can save the settings they use most as a preset and load it on any device.
  • On a computer, the work panel on the right now follows the page as you scroll.

28 September 2026

  • The next picture starts with the boxes you just confirmed, so a run of similar shots does not need the boxes placed again.
  • The video tool now plays its sample clip as soon as it opens.

25 September 2026

  • Added the About, How to use and Use cases pages.
  • The video crop tool is now public.

13 September 2026

  • DetailCrop launched.
