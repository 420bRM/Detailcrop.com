This page lists everything DetailCrop (detailcrop.com) collects. We keep it to what the site needs, and nothing is left off this list.

1. Your images are never collected

The images you crop, and their file names, are handled entirely inside your browser. There is no code that sends them anywhere, and the server refuses unread any request over 1 KB (64 KB for a signed-in member's preset).

2. What we process, and why

Every visitor — anonymous usage stats

  • What: a random ID your browser makes for itself, visit count, interface language, country, the referring site (domain only), desktop or phone, the settings you pick (ratio, output size, format, quality and so on), image sizes and counts, which features you use, how often you save and load presets and how many you hold, and the purpose you choose if you answer the one question after an export
  • Why: to see which parts of the tool get used, so we can improve it
  • No name, email, IP address or image goes into the stats. The "turn off" switch at the foot of the page stops them, and the browser's Do Not Track setting is honoured.

Signed-in members — presets

  • What: your Google account's email address and account ID, when you joined and last signed in, the presets you save (a name and settings — ratio, output size, format, quality, file-name pattern, feed split, box positions), and what keeps you signed in
  • Why: signing you in, saving and loading your presets, and answering you if you write to us
  • Google passes on your email address only — no name, no photo. We send no marketing email.
  • What keeps you signed in stays in your browser; the server keeps only a one-way hash of it.

3. How long it is kept

  • Account (email address, account ID) and presets: until you delete your account. The email address and account ID are then erased at once.
  • Preset settings after an account is deleted: with the preset name, file-name pattern and every account detail removed, so they cannot be tied to anyone, the settings themselves (ratio, output size, format, quality, box positions) are kept as statistics to improve the tool, with no set end date.
  • Sign-in: 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Anonymous stats: they identify no one, and are kept to improve the tool, with no set end date.

4. Sharing

We do not give your information to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. Services that store it, and where

Two US companies' services run the site, so your information is stored and processed outside your country.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — serves the site and stores accounts, presets and stats; everything in section 2, sent over the network as you use the site, kept as in section 3.
  • Google LLC (USA) — confirms your Google sign-in. When you pick your account in the sign-in window, Google passes your email address to DetailCrop. What happens inside that window falls under Google's privacy policy.

Every cropping feature works without signing in, and the stats can be switched off.

6. What we keep in your browser

No cookies. The page uses your browser's local storage for your last settings, language, the random stats ID and whether stats are off, whether you have seen the walkthrough, and your sign-in. Clearing your browser data clears them. Google's sign-in window may set Google's own cookies.

7. Your rights

  • Signed in, you can see your email and presets on screen, and rename or delete any preset.
  • Open "Presets" in Settings and press "Delete account" to erase your account and every preset in it.
  • For anything else — access, correction, deletion, or stopping processing — write to the address below.

8. Under 14

You must be 14 or older to sign in. An account we learn belongs to someone younger is deleted.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses Google's signed proof of identity, checked against Google's public keys, so no password is ever handled. Requests from other sites are refused and request rates are limited.

10. Contact

Person responsible for personal information: the operator of DetailCrop — contact@detailcrop.com

11. Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with the date they take effect.
